Sign in for your app

Who your workspace's app runs as when Build.Rite runs it on this PC, and the login its backend calls go out with. Saved on this PC only, never in a project.

Who
Fusion login

Used where VB Studio uses its stored credential: the backend calls the app makes through its service connections (Fusion REST, BI Publisher reports). Without it those calls are refused and their lists stay empty. The password is encrypted with your Windows account.

Other backend logins

For a backend that signs in with its own credential, not the Fusion one — e.g. Oracle Integration (OIC). Without this, its calls borrow the Fusion login and the backend answers 401. Backend name is the one in services/catalog.json (for OIC it is ics); you can also use its credential id. Passwords are encrypted with your Windows account, saved on this PC only.
